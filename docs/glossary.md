# Netrek Glossary

Key terms from the original Netrek community. Useful for naming things in code, UI text, and understanding game culture.

## Game Modes & States

- **T-Mode (Tournament Mode)**: Competitive mode, activates when both teams have 4+ players. Bombing and planet capture only possible in T-Mode.
- **INL**: International Netrek League. Timed competitive format (90 min, 11-8 win condition, 30-min sudden death OT).
- **Bronco**: Standard Netrek server type. Our target implementation.
- **Chaos**: Variant with high refuel rates, free plasmas, Galaxy class ships. Not our target.

## Ship & Combat Terms

- **Ogg**: Cloaked suicide attack. Cloak, approach, uncloak adjacent, tractor-phaser-torp until target dies.
- **Buttorp**: Firing torpedoes rearward while fleeing. Exploits pursuer's momentum.
- **Plock / Phaser lock**: Hitting a cloaked ship with a phaser, revealing their exact position.
- **Det**: Detonating torpedoes (yours or enemy's). Costs 100 fuel, heats weapons.
- **Mutual**: Both ships killing each other in the same explosion.
- **Cripple**: Ship with heavy hull damage, reduced max speed.
- **Redlining**: Flying with engine temp near/above 95. Risk of burnout.
- **Doosh**: Killing a carrier loaded with armies.
- **Free beer**: An easy kill target.
- **Chain reaction**: Ship explosion killing nearby ships in succession.

## Army & Planet Terms

- **Pregnant / Loaded**: Carrying armies. "Loaded" = 5+ armies.
- **+, ++**: Indicates carrying armies. More plusses = more armies.
- **Kids / Pops**: Armies.
- **Flat**: Planet with 4 or fewer armies (can't be bombed, can't pick up from).
- **AGRI**: Agricultural planet. Grows armies faster. Strategically vital.
- **Rock**: Planet with no special facilities. Least important.
- **Core planets**: Homeworld and four nearest planets. Some servers award double DI for capturing these.
- **Cored**: Team reduced to 5 core planets only.
- **Take / Scum**: Capture a planet.
- **Hard-take**: Taking and reinforcing a planet in a single trip.
- **Part-drop**: Partially weakening an enemy planet without capturing it.
- **Reinforce**: Beaming armies onto owned planets to strengthen them.

## Player Roles

- **Taker**: Primary planet-capturing role.
- **Escort**: Protecting army carriers en route to planets.
- **Clear**: Killing defenders so teammates can bomb/capture.
- **Scout bombing**: Bombing deep in enemy territory.
- **Space controlling**: Dogfighting at front, escorting, point defense.

## Team Tactics

- **Wave / Synch**: Coordinated multi-ship attack, especially on starbases.
- **Decoy**: Drawing enemy attention from the real attack.
- **Fake / Cut**: Feinting toward one planet, then cutting to another.
- **Shadow**: Following an enemy just outside weapon range.
- **LPS (Last Planet Stand)**: Final defensive position against genocide.

## Scoring

- **DI**: Destruction Inflicted rating. Calculated from planets, bombing, offense, multiplied by hours played.
- **Offense / Defense / Bombing / Planet ratings**: Performance stats. 1.00 = average.
- **ACC**: Actual Carriers Created. Kills that led to army pickups.
- **Kill-flat (KF)**: Team where no ships have kills (except maybe starbase).

## Culture

- **Clue**: Strategic understanding and skill. "Having clue" = being a competent player.
- **Clueless**: Derogatory term for unskilled players.
- **Ensign Clueless**: New player. Can still help team through positioning and distraction.
- **Newbie**: Beginning player.
- **Twink**: Persistently poor player.
- **Scum**: Context-dependent. Can mean "specialist" (planet scum = planet-focused player) or genuinely bad behavior (slot scum = AFK player wasting a slot).
- **God**: Server administrator.
