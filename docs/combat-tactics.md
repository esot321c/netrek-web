# Netrek Combat Tactics Reference

Compiled from original Netrek community guides. Use this to inform combat feel, AI behavior, and tutorial content.

## Core Combat Principles

- Phasers are instantaneous hitscan — cannot be dodged, but damage drops with distance
- Torpedo clouds (8 max in flight per player) travel over time and can be dodged or detonated
- "Buttorping" — firing torps rearward while running, exploits enemy momentum
- Team detonations create overlapping damage zones

## Speed & Turning

- Each additional warp halves turning rate
- Warp 3 is minimum speed for tight turning in combat
- Warp 4 is max recommended combat speed (CA)
- Experienced players constantly vary speed to be unpredictable
- High speed = committed trajectory (intentional design)
- Speed directly affects dodge ability: slow = maneuverable, fast = straight-line

## Engagement Tactics

### Approach

- Never charge straight at an enemy at max warp — you'll eat torps before you can turn
- Approach on diagonal vectors, weaving side-to-side
- Warp 7 approach (not max) allows better torpedo evasion

### Dogfighting

- Optimal phaser range: 30-40 damage points (medium distance)
- 20-point phaser damage is considered minimum useful engagement range
- Execute near-360-degree rotations to dodge while maintaining position
- Weave and spin only effective at warp 4-5 or lower
- Fire partial torp clouds to predicted positions, then secondary volleys to anticipated dodge trajectories
- "Wiggle the mouse a little" when firing torps for spread patterns

### Tractor/Pressor

- Pull enemies into torpedo clouds
- Push opponents away during high-warp charges
- Yo-yoing (alternating tractor/pressor) throws opponents off balance
- Both consume significant fuel (200 fuel/second)

## The Ogg (Suicide Attack)

Standard attack pattern against key targets:

1. Cloak before entering enemy tactical view
2. Approach at warp 7 (not max — need turning ability)
3. Get very close (~half inch on screen)
4. Tractor-phaser-torp-torp-torp until target dies
5. Multiple oggers should uncloak simultaneously from different directions

### Starbase Ogging

- Requires multiple coordinated waves
- All oggers uncloak at same time
- Approach from different angles
- Rule of thumb: need 2 oggers per 1 defender
- Destroy docked ships first (worth 100 damage to base + removes defenders)

## Defense Tactics

- Position between enemy and objective
- Target cloaked ships and army carriers first
- Tractor carriers out of orbit to prevent army deployment
- Detonate incoming enemy torpedoes ('d' key)
- Incoming torp defense: detonating early reduces damage taken
- Fly perpendicular to enemy torp vectors to dodge

## Cloaking Combat

- Short delay between uncloaking and being able to fire — exploit this on defense
- Immediate pressor activation slows uncloaking attackers
- Cloaked enemies show as "??" on galactic map only (with random position offset)
- Phaser lock reveals exact position of cloaked ship

## Key Terminology

- **Doosh**: Killing an important carrier loaded with armies
- **Mutual**: Both ships killing each other in the same explosion
- **Cripple**: Heavily damaged ship with reduced max speed
- **Redlining**: Flying with engine temp above 95 degrees (risky)
- **Split damage**: Taking 1 hull per 2 shield points for optimal repair efficiency
- **Phaser lock (plock)**: Hitting a cloaked ship with phaser to reveal position
- **Free beer**: An easy-to-kill target
- **Chain reaction**: Ship explosion killing nearby ships in succession
